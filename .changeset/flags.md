Flags, drawn as circles as in ggflags. `@samizdata/mozaic/flags` exports the square flags of flag-icons (MIT) as SVG markup, one per upper-case ISO code (`MD`, `RO`, `EU`, and Eurostat's `EL` and `UK`); import only the ones a chart uses. New marks `Flag` (one flag at a pixel position) and `Flags` (a flag per datum at its x/y). `Beeswarm`, `BarChart` (between each label and its bar) and `ScatterChart` take a `flag` accessor.
