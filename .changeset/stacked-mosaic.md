Two recipes for parts of a whole: `StackedBarChart` (100% stacked bars with the series name and share inside segments wide enough to hold them, optional brackets above the first bar grouping neighbouring series, a reference line and a legend of groups) and `MosaicChart` (a Marimekko chart: 100% stacked rows as tall as their totals, rows sorted by the share of chosen series, row labels nudged apart and series named inside their largest cell). Both can be ejected with `mozaic eject`.
